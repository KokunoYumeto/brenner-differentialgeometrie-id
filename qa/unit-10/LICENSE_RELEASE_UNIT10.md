## Media and other components

Media do not inherit a blanket repository license. Every admitted image and
interactive asset retains its own creator, source revision, and license as
recorded in `authority/brenner_media_rights_manifest.csv`,
`source/unit_media.json`, and the readers' attribution surfaces. The cumulative
source set through Unit 10 includes public-domain, CC BY, and CC BY-SA
components from multiple license versions. Reusers must follow each
component's recorded terms. Units 9–10 add `Tangentialvektor.svg`, `Tangent
bundle.svg`, and `Torus vectors oblique.jpg`; the first two are public-domain
files and the third remains CC BY-SA 3.0 by RokerHRO.

Build, backend, HTML, and QA scripts written specifically for this edition are
released under CC BY-SA 4.0 unless a file states otherwise. No CC BY-NC-SA
source prose is incorporated into the Brenner-derived reader. Any later
original bridge module will identify its authorship and license explicitly.
